Designer. Observer. Serial Overthinker.

I’m Eeshitvaa — a product and interaction designer interested in the tiny moments where people pause, hesitate, misunderstand something, or finally get it. I like turning those moments into interfaces that feel clearer, calmer and a little more human.

When I’m not doing that, I’m probably making a strange little game, rearranging type, reading something, or noticing the kerning on a street sign.
